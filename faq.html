Getting started

Does AAAstreamz come with channels?

No. AAAstreamz is a player. You need your own IPTV subscription from a provider, who gives you either an Xtream Codes login (server, username and password) or an M3U playlist link. You're responsible for choosing a legitimate provider.

Is it free?

Yes. There are no ads and no subscription. If you enjoy it, you can leave a tip on Ko-fi, but that's entirely optional.

Which devices does it work on?

Android-based TV devices running Android 7.0 or newer: Amazon Fire TV (Stick, Cube), Google TV, Android TV (onn, Nvidia Shield, and TVs from brands like Philips, TCL, Hisense and Sony), plus Android phones and tablets.

Is there an iPhone or Apple TV version?

An iPhone version is being tested and isn't available yet. There are no plans for Apple TV right now.

Installing and updating

How do I install it?

With the free Downloader app on your TV and the code 4827015. The step-by-step guide takes about five minutes, and you only do it once.

It says "App not installed". What do I do?

Use the Downloader code 4827015, not a separate 64-bit download. That version installs on every TV box, including many TVs that run 32-bit Android on a 64-bit chip (common on Philips, TCL and Hisense). Also check you have enough free storage.

Android or Play Protect warns me. Is it safe?

The warning appears because AAAstreamz isn't installed from the Play Store, not because anything is wrong with it. Choose More details › Install anyway. The app is signed, and every update comes from the same place. See the privacy policy for exactly what it sends and where.

How do updates work?

When a new version comes out, the app shows an Update available pop-up when it starts. Press Update and it installs itself. Your login, favorites and settings are kept. You can also check yourself in Settings › About › Check for updates. See What's new for every version's changes.

Your data

Where are my login details stored?

Only on your TV box. They're sent to your IPTV provider and nowhere else. Problem reports and crash reports never include your username or password.

Can I use more than one provider?

Yes. Add each one in Settings › Playlists › + Add playlist and switch from the playlist button at the top of the screen. Each keeps its own favorites. See Playlists.

How do I move everything to a new TV box?

Save a backup with your phone, then restore it on the new box. On the new box's login screen, choose Have a backup from another TV? Restore it. Your playlists, settings and favorites come across and you don't retype anything. See Backup & restore.

What happens if I uninstall?

Everything the app stored on that box is deleted: logins, favorites, settings and recordings. Save a backup first if you want to keep them.

Watching

Channels or movies buffer, or movies take a long time to start

Run Settings › Speed test. It checks your home internet and your provider separately. If your internet is fast but the provider is slow, the problem is on the provider's end. Some internet providers also slow down these servers: if things play smoothly on a VPN, that's what's happening. For live channels, try Settings › Playback › Buffer size › Stable.

A channel or movie won't play at all

Try another one. Providers sometimes have dead links, and that's outside the app's control. If nothing plays, check your subscription is active with your provider.

There's sound but the picture is black

Go to Settings › Playback › Decoding and choose Software. Some devices' hardware decoders have trouble with certain streams.

The TV guide is empty or at the wrong times

Try Settings › Data › Refresh EPG. If shows are listed at the wrong time, use Settings › Interface › Guide time shift. Some providers don't supply guide data for every channel.

Can I record?

Yes. Hold OK on a live channel and choose Record. Recordings are under Recordings in the top bar. See Recording.

Can I stop kids from watching some categories?

Yes. Set a PIN in Settings › Parental control and lock the categories you choose, separately for Live TV, Movies and Series.

Getting help

How do I report a problem?

In the app, go to Settings › About › Report a problem, add a short note and send it. You'll get a report number. Email us at aaastreamz@gmail.com and mention that number, and we'll see exactly what happened. Reports never include your username or password.
