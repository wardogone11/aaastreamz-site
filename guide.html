How to use AAAstreamz

Everything the app can do, using a normal TV remote.

1. Logging in

Pick one:

  • Xtream Codes: your provider's server address, username and password
  • M3U URL: a single playlist link

Tick Remember on this device so you don't have to type it again. Your username and password stay on your device. Have more than one provider? Log in with each one and switch between them under Settings › Accounts.

2. Finding your way around

Across the top: Live TV, Movies, Series, plus Guide, Ask, Recordings and Settings.

  • Categories are the row of buttons under the tabs. Scroll left and right through them.
  • ★ Favorites holds everything you've starred. 🕘 Recent (Live TV) shows the last channels you watched.
  • Search filters the list you're looking at by name.

Remote tips

  • Hold Up/Down to scroll faster. Fast-forward/Rewind (or Channel Up/Down) jump a whole page.
  • Back inside a list takes you up to the categories. From the main screen, press Back twice to leave the app.
  • Menu (☰) on Fire TV remotes jumps to the top bar.

3. Watching Live TV

  • Press OK on a channel to play it in the small window. Press OK again to go fullscreen.
  • Scrolling the list doesn't change the channel. Only OK does.
  • In fullscreen: Up/Down changes the channel, OK pauses and plays, hold OK (or ☰ on Fire TV) chooses the audio track, and Back returns to the list.
  • Hold OK on a channel in the list for Add to Favorites, Record or Hide Channel.
  • When you start the app it goes straight back to the last channel you watched, full screen. To keep it in the small window, change Settings › Interface › Last channel plays. To open the channel list with nothing playing, change Settings › Interface › On startup.

The Guide

Guide opens a full TV listing of what's on now and later. The video shrinks to the corner while you browse. Pick a show to tune to that channel. If the times are off by an hour or more, use Settings › Interface › Guide time shift.

4. Movies & Series

  • Choose a movie to play it, or choose a series to pick a season and episode.
  • Playback resumes where you left off.
  • While playing: Left/Right skips back or forward 10 seconds, OK pauses and plays, and Up/Down or hold OK opens Audio & Subtitles.

5. Ask: search by voice or words

Open Ask and type or speak what you want: "football", "something funny", "cooking", or a channel name like "CNN".

Hands-free: start with a play word, like "play CNN", "watch ESPN 2" or "put on the Weather Channel". AAAstreamz finds the channel and starts it after a 3-second countdown. Press OK to start right away, or Back to cancel. If it isn't sure, it shows a "Did you mean…" list.

To use your voice, press the microphone on your remote or on the on-screen keyboard.

6. Use your phone as a remote

Open Ask. It shows a QR code and a web address. Open it on your phone (on the same Wi-Fi as the TV) and you get:

  • Arrows, OK and Back that work anywhere in the app
  • Play/Pause, Rewind, Fast-forward, Volume and Mute
  • A keyboard and mic for searching (much easier than the TV keyboard)

The page keeps working until you close the app.

7. Recording

Hold OK on a live channel and choose Record to record what's on now. Find your recordings (and stop or delete them) under Recordings in the top bar. Choose where they're saved in Settings › Recordings.

8. Customizing

  • Edit channels / Edit list: reorder or hide channels, movies and categories you don't want
  • Settings › Parental control: set a PIN and lock categories behind it
  • Settings › Interface: 12h/24h guide times, row size, accent color, and what happens on startup
  • Settings › Data: refresh the guide or the channel list if something looks out of date

9. Updates

When a new version is out, the app shows a pop-up when it starts. Choose Update and confirm Android's install screen. You'll see what's new each time. To check yourself, go to Settings › About › Check for updates, where your version number is shown too.

10. If something isn't working

ProblemTry this
Sound but a black screenSettings › Playback › Decoding › Software
Channels stall or bufferRun Settings › Speed test. Then try Settings › Playback › Buffer size › Stable, or Live stream format › TS
A channel won't startTry another channel. Some provider links are dead, and that's on the provider's end
Guide is empty or wrongSettings › Data › Refresh EPG, or use Guide time shift
A channel is missingSettings › Data › Refresh playlist

The first load of a very large channel list can take a few seconds. That's normal.

Speed test

Go to Settings › Speed test › Run speed test. It takes about 25 seconds and checks your provider's server and your home internet separately. HD needs about 10 Mbps and 4K about 25 Mbps. If your internet is fast but your provider is slow, the buffering is on the provider's end, not your Wi-Fi.

Report a problem

Go to Settings › About › Report a problem. Add a note about what happened (optional) and send. We get your app version, device and a log of what went wrong, but never your username or password. You'll get a report number. Mention it if you contact us. Crashes are reported automatically.
