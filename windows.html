AAAstreamz for Windows

Winbloze Edition. The same player you use on your TV, now on your Windows 10 or 11 PC.

⬇ Download for Windows

Windows 10 or 11, 64 bit. About 120 MB.

What you get

📺

Live TV + Guide

Browse by category, star favorites and see what's on now and later. Use the mouse and keyboard, or your phone.

🎬

Movies & Series

Pick up where you left off, with a seek bar and a Continue watching shelf. Audio and subtitle choices show up when a stream has them.

🧩

Multi-View

Watch two live channels side by side. Click one to hear it, double-click to watch it alone.

⏺

Record

Record now, schedule a show, or record every episode. Recordings get a picture, play with a working seek bar, and keep going when you close the window to the tray.

📱

Phone remote

Scan the code and your phone becomes a remote, a keyboard and a TV guide for the PC.

🔄

Updates itself

When a new version is out, the app asks if you want it. It never restarts in the middle of a recording.

How to install

Download the file above and double-click it. Windows may say it protected your PC because this installer is new and not yet widely downloaded. Choose More info, then Run anyway.

Pick a folder, or keep the one it suggests. Open AAAstreamz Winbloze Edition from the desktop or the Start menu, then sign in with your IPTV details, the same ones you use on the TV.

The first time it opens, Windows may ask if the app can use your home network. Choose Allow on private networks if you want the phone remote.

To remove it, go to Windows Settings › Apps › Installed apps and uninstall it. Your recordings stay where you saved them.

Good to know

AAAstreamz is a player. It doesn't come with channels or playlists. You need your own IPTV service.

Some 4K titles can be choppy on PCs that can't decode them in hardware, so the 4K lists are hidden on Windows.

Scheduled recordings only run while the app is open. Closing the window sends it to the tray by the clock, and it keeps recording there. Use Quit in the tray menu to close it completely.
