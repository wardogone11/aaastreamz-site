What it does

Everything is built to be quick with a TV remote. No tiny buttons and no endless menus.

📺

Live TV + Guide

Browse by category, star favorites, jump back to recent channels, and see what's on now and later in a full TV guide.

🎬

Movies & Series

Pick up where you left off, skip 10 seconds with the remote, and switch audio language and subtitles.

🎙️

Say "play CNN"

Search by voice or words. Start with "play" or "watch" and the channel starts by itself.

AAAstreamz is a player only. It doesn't come with any channels or content. You need your own IPTV subscription (Xtream Codes login or M3U playlist link).

Install on your TV

You do this on the TV box itself with the remote, and only once. After that, updates install from inside the app.

Get the free "Downloader" app

On the TV, open the app store (Amazon Appstore on Fire TV, Google Play on Google TV, Android TV, onn and Shield), search Downloader (the orange app by AFTVnews) and install it.

Let Downloader install apps

  • Fire TV: Settings › My Fire TV › Developer Options › Install unknown apps › Downloader: On.
    No Developer Options? Go to Settings › My Fire TV › About and click the device name 7 times.
  • Google TV / Android TV / onn / Shield: Settings › System › About, click Android TV OS build 7 times. Then Settings › Apps › Security & restrictions › Unknown sources › Downloader: On.
